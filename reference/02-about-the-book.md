# About the book

*Scaling Silicon Valley Style* (Second Edition) is Roland Siebelink's playbook for companies past product-market fit. Roland grew three startups from 10 to 1,000 people, three years each.

## The premise

Most startup advice is about finding product-market fit. Very little covers what comes after, which is where most scaleups stall. Growth exposes problems the founding team never had to solve: too many priorities, hires who wait for the founder, sales channels that don't pay back. The book's goal for a scaleup is product-market dominance: the biggest share of its market, recognized as number one, with a moat.

## What Volume 1 covers

- **Beyond product-market fit.** Why scaleups fail after finding fit, and why there is no standard playbook.
- **The Scaleup Roadmap.** The six stages, from Applicant to Graduate (see `01-scaleup-roadmap.md`).
- **Applicants: mastering customers.** Painkillers versus vitamins, real traction, readiness for a Series A.
- **Freshmen: mastering distribution.** Testing product-market fit, core decisions, sales channels that pay back, readiness for a Series B.
- **Sophomores: mastering deepening.** Accountability, the quarterly rhythm, crossing into the mainstream market, focusing on profitable segments, readiness for a Series C.
- **Conclusion.** Five steps any leadership team can start this quarter.
