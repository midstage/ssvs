# Connecting and billing

## What connecting does

The free `ssvs-start-here` skill works on its own. Connecting adds the full SSVS kit: one skill per framework in the book, served live from Midstage so you always have the current version.

## What you need

- **Claude:** the web or desktop app, or Claude Code. Add `https://skills.midstage.ac/mcp` as a connector and sign in with your connect code. Support for ChatGPT and other AI assistants is coming soon.
- **A subscription.** USD 19 a month, paid through Stripe. Your welcome email has a link that gets you a fresh connect code whenever you need one.

## What we store

- **Your email and name**, as you gave them at Stripe checkout.
- **Your subscription status**, as reported by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
- **Which kit tools you use, and when.** We use this to improve the kit.

Your conversations stay in your own Claude. We only receive what Claude sends to the kit's tools, such as which skill to load.

## Cancelling

Cancel any time online at https://skills.midstage.ac/cancel, or email support@midstage.ac. Access stops when the subscription ends.
